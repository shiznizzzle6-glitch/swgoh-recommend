Counter builder

Countering isn't about memorising which character beats which. It's about reading what the enemy's text actually does, then bringing the mechanic that breaks it. Paste any enemy ability or modifier below — or pick a Conquest trial — and this reads it for you, then finds which of your units bring each tool and quotes the line that proves it.

The method — five questions, every time

What's their engine?
Find the sentence that makes them dangerous — the crit, the stack, the revive. Everything else is decoration.
What feeds it?
Engines need fuel: buffs gained, stacks built, turns taken. Cut the fuel and the engine stalls without you out-damaging it.
What does it punish?
The most important question. Some enemies profit from your debuffs or your chip damage. Bringing your usual squad can actively lose you the fight.
Which mechanic breaks it?
Name the tool, not the character: dispel, Buff Immunity, Ability Block, Turn Meter removal, crit denial, prevent-revive.
Can I actually field it?
A counter on a G8 unit that dies first isn't a counter. Check relic/gear, and whether the ability needs a zeta you haven't learned.
